Terms & House Rules

The Fine Print, Minus the Boring

Cancellation

Free cancellation if you booked 24 hours or more before your arrival day. Cancellations inside 24 hours of arrival may be charged the first night. No-shows are charged the first night.

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in from 2:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM. Reception in Addis Ababa is staffed 24 hours, so late arrivals are fine — just let us know.

Payment Options

You've got two simple ways to pay (all prices in USD):

  • Reserve now, pay at check-in. Hold your bed online and pay the full amount when you arrive — cash or card both work.
  • Pay online in full at booking and save 10%. Pay everything up front when you book and we knock 10% off your stay.

House Rules

  • Be kind — to guests, staff and neighbours. Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
  • Quiet hours in dorms from 11:00 PM. The party continues in the bar or garden.
  • No smoking in rooms; use the garden smoking area.
  • Guests must be 18+ or accompanied by an adult.
  • Outside visitors must sign in at reception and leave by 10:00 PM.
  • Keep valuables in the lockers provided — we can't be responsible for items left out.
  • Damage to property is charged to the person or group responsible.
  • Respect local laws and customs — you're a guest in the neighbourhood too.
