Why Vervet?

Vervet monkeys are endemic to the eastern part of Africa — curious, playful, social and rarely found alone. They live in groups, look out for each other, and somehow always seem to find a little chaos along the way.

That felt like the perfect spirit for a hostel.

Mad Vervet started in Addis Ababa in 2019 with a simple idea: create a place where travellers could do more than just sleep. A place to meet people, share stories, discover Ethiopia, have a drink, eat together and feel at home — even when you’re far from home.

Our Nairobi home came next, bringing the same spirit to a different city, with more garden space, outdoor hangouts and plenty of room to slow down.

We’re still guided by the same idea we started with:

Keep it welcoming. Keep it social. Keep it affordable. And whenever possible, don’t let anyone eat dinner alone.
