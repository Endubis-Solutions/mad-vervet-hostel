Why the Monkey?

The vervet monkey is East Africa's most social animal — loud, curious, mischievous and always in a group. That's exactly the hostel we set out to build: a place where solo travellers land and instantly have a crew.

Mad Vervet started in Addis Ababa's Bole neighbourhood with a simple idea — budget beds, a proper bar, and something happening every single night. Our Nairobi home in Lavington followed, trading the karaoke room for garden picnics and a terrace bar.

Everything we do runs on three rules: keep it cheap, keep it social, and never let anyone eat dinner alone.
